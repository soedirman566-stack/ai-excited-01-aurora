# Aurora Surfer

A joyful penguin in a streaming red scarf rides a coral surfboard down the face of a towering wave woven from aurora-borealis light — ribbons of electric green, teal and violet curl like a breaking barrel under a starry indigo sky, the foam at the crest bursting into spray that dissolves into stars, with a bright full moon watching over the night sea.

Rendered procedurally by `generate.py` (Python + PIL/numpy, drawn in layered passes at 2x supersampling and downscaled to 1024x1024). Run `python3 generate.py` to regenerate `entry.png`.
